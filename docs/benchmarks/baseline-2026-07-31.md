## Methodology

- Enqueue latency: client-side POST round-trip time (curl timing) at concurrency 50.
- Completion latency: end-to-end time-in-system reconstructed from the Redis `metrics:total`
  (arrival) and `metrics:completed` (completion) counter curves sampled at ~50ms, assuming
  FIFO completion per priority queue. Resolution is limited to the sampling grid; for very fast
  jobs (<100ms) treat sub-sample precision as approximate.
- Sustained jobs/s: completed count / (last completion crossing - first arrival crossing).
- Error rate, DLQ growth, and unfinished count are exact (polled per job).
